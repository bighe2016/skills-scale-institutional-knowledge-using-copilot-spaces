# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Executive Sponsor provides strategic direction, secures resources, and helps remove organizational blockers that could slow or derail the project. They act as a senior champion for the initiative and provide sponsorship at key decision points.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priority and strategic alignment
- Sponsor budget, staffing, and executive support
- Resolve escalations that require cross-functional or leadership intervention
- Review progress against outcomes and major milestones
- Support go/no-go decisions and major scope trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains aligned with business priorities
- Remove risk to delivery caused by organizational friction
- Support successful adoption and measurable value realization

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with the Product Manager to confirm business priorities and goals
- Partners with the Project Manager on escalations, decisions, and dependency resolution
- Provides strategic context to stakeholders and senior leadership
- Supports the team while preserving accountability of the PM and delivery leads

### Typical Communication
- Executive check-ins and milestone reviews
- Sponsor briefings and escalation updates
- Decision log and major risk communication

---

## Project Coordinator / Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Project Coordinator supports the Project Manager by tracking schedules, actions, dependencies, and project documentation. This role helps keep the team aligned and informed without replacing the PM’s ownership of delivery coordination.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project calendars, action trackers, and meeting notes
- Log decisions, risks, and follow-up actions
- Track dependencies across teams and milestones
- Support meeting facilitation and status reporting
- Help distribute status updates and align workstreams

### Goals
- Keep project information organized and accessible
- Reduce missed handoffs and communication gaps
- Improve visibility into delivery progress and blockers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports the Project Manager in planning, reporting, and coordination
- Works with Developers, Product Managers, and QA to gather updates and track dependencies
- Helps the broader team stay aligned on deliverables and decisions

### Typical Communication
- Standup support and status tracking
- Status summaries and dependency updates
- Action-item follow-up and meeting coordination

---

## UX / Research Lead

### Role Summary
The UX / Research Lead helps the team understand user needs, validate assumptions, and ensure the planned solution is usable, accessible, and aligned with customer expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define research plans and discovery activities
- Gather user feedback, usability insights, and pain points
- Translate findings into user needs, requirements, and design recommendations
- Collaborate with Product Managers on prioritization and acceptance criteria
- Support design decisions and usability validation

### Goals
- Improve customer value and experience quality
- Reduce unnecessary rework caused by weak assumptions
- Ensure usability and accessibility are built into delivery decisions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape problem definition and success criteria
- Provides input to Developers and Technical Leads on user experience and implementation constraints
- Collaborates with QA on usability testing and acceptance validation
- Supports the PM with risk identification related to customer experience or adoption

### Typical Communication
- Research summaries and user insights
- Design review input and usability findings
- Collaboration with product and delivery planning

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead / Architect guides the technical direction of the solution, helps manage design trade-offs, and reduces implementation risk by aligning engineering decisions with product goals and project constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design direction
- Guide integration decisions and interface design
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and performance concerns
- Support developers with technical guidance and design reviews
- Help sequence work and coordinate architecture decisions across teams

### Goals
- Reduce technical complexity and delivery risk
- Keep implementation aligned with architecture and quality standards
- Enable scalable, maintainable solutions

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to support implementation quality and design consistency
- Aligns with Product Managers on trade-offs between scope, timeline, and technical feasibility
- Partners with the Project Manager to sequence work and surface dependencies
- Shares technical risks with QA and stakeholders where relevant to release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Risk and dependency discussions
- Implementation trade-off guidance during planning and delivery

---

## QA / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The QA / Test Lead ensures quality expectations are defined, tested, and validated before release. This role helps the team reduce defects, improve confidence, and make quality and acceptance criteria explicit.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and quality gates
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect triage
- Validate acceptance criteria with product and stakeholder expectations
- Identify quality risks and release blockers
- Support regression and smoke-test readiness

### Goals
- Reduce risk of release defects and quality gaps
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before handoff
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and operational readiness

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to define test coverage and validate fixes
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on readiness, risk, and milestone quality gates
- Informs stakeholders and leadership when quality concerns may affect release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, QA milestones, and defect triage
- Quality readiness reports and release validation updates
- Escalations for acceptance or regression risks

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Privacy Lead helps the team identify and manage security, privacy, and compliance requirements throughout the project lifecycle. This role ensures the solution is designed and delivered with risk controls and appropriate governance.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security and privacy risks early in planning
- Define controls, policies, and compliance requirements
- Review architecture, data handling, and sensitive workflows
- Partner with Developers and QA on implementation and validation
- Coordinate escalation and response for security or privacy incidents

### Goals
- Reduce risk of security and privacy failures
- Align delivery with policy and compliance obligations
- Improve trust and resilience in production environments

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers on requirements, trade-offs, and risk implications
- Collaborates with Technical Leads and Developers on secure design and implementation
- Works with QA to validate controls and security readiness
- Supports the Project Manager in escalations and readiness decisions

### Typical Communication
- Security review check-ins and threat or compliance updates
- Risk assessments and remediation tracking
- Incident escalation and policy guidance

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead helps the project define how success will be measured, how data will be collected, and how outcomes will be validated against the original goal.

### Responsibilities
- Define instrumentation, metrics, and reporting needs
- Support tracking of adoption, usage, and business outcomes
- Help validate whether the solution is producing the expected effect
- Work with Product Managers on success metrics and analysis
- Coordinate with Developers and QA on implementation and verification of metrics

### Goals
- Create evidence-based decision making
- Improve visibility into product impact and adoption
- Ensure outcomes are measurable and actionable

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to define measurable success
- Partners with Developers to instrument workflows and validate data quality
- Supports QA with data validation and reporting accuracy
- Helps the Project Manager report progress against milestones and outcome measures

### Typical Communication
- Dashboards, reports, and metric reviews
- Outcome tracking and analysis updates
- Recommendations based on observed product or user data

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Different projects may include some or all of these roles depending on scope, risk, and organizational needs.
- Clear role boundaries help teams improve accountability, reduce ambiguity, and make project execution more predictable.
